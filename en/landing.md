## What you will make

Build an endless clicker where players tap one clear thing, buy upgrades, and watch the numbers grow faster and faster.

Click the green flag to start, then click the pizza to earn pizzas. Buy equipment to make each click worth more, and hire helpers to earn pizzas automatically.
