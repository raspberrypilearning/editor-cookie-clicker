## Paint a shop backdrop

The game works, but it is still plain. Give it a proper shop front.

<h2 class="c-project-heading--explainer">What you need to do</h2>

## Step 1

Hover over **Choose a Backdrop** at the bottom right and click **Paint** to make a blank backdrop.

## Step 2

Use the **Text** tool in the paint editor to add labels and instructions.

The example pizza shop uses "Click the pizza to make one!", "Hire Help Here!", and "Equipment store".

Use your own words and colours to suit your game's theme.

## Now run your code

You'll arrange the sprites and readouts on top of the backdrop next.
