## Make it your own

You've built a complete endless clicker. Now customise it and add your own ideas.

<h2 class="c-project-heading--explainer">What you need to do</h2>

You don't have to try everything — pick the changes that sound the most fun.

- Can you change the clicker, equipment, helpers, backdrop, and sounds to give the game a completely new theme?
- Can you balance the prices and rewards so each upgrade arrives at the right time?
- Can you add another piece of equipment with its own price and multiplier?
- Can you add a third kind of helper that earns a different amount every second?
- Can you change the win condition to suit the pace of your game?
- Can you invent another animation for a sprite when it is clicked or unlocked?

## Now run your code

Play your finished game from the beginning and check that every upgrade can be bought, the helper rate stays correct, and the player can still win.

Remember to save your project.
